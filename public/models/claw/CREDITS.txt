Claw machine prize models (prizes.glb)

Made from these packs by Kenney (https://kenney.nl), licence CC0 1.0 (public domain):
- Cube Pets 1.0: animal-bunny, animal-cat, animal-panda, animal-chick, animal-pig, animal-penguin
- Holiday Kit: present-a-cube, present-b-round
- Toy Car Kit: vehicle-monster-truck, vehicle-racer
- Car Kit: tractor-shovel, garbage-truck, tractor, firetruck, police, ambulance

Each model is a top-level node named by its prize id (see src/play/claw/prizes.ts), scaled to fit a
1 x 1 x 1 box (centred on x/z, bottom at y = 0). Merged into one file with gltf-transform: textures
embedded and deduplicated, vertices welded, attributes quantized (KHR_mesh_quantization, no decoder
needed): see scripts/build-claw-models.mjs. The claw, the beach ball, the star, the airplane and the helicopter are drawn in code.
