# BrickTown — 4-lane avenues + horizon and time of day (Plan)

User decisions (2026-10-02):
- **Roads**: two kinds of road. 🛣️ 4-lane avenue (2 cells wide, the default brush) and a 2-lane street (1 cell, today's road). Roads in old saves stay as streets. The sample town switches to avenues plus a few side streets. NPC cars use the right lanes for each kind.
- **Horizon and time**: distant LEGO-style mountains and forest around the horizon. Four time-of-day presets picked by hand (morning, noon, sunset, night), plus an optional automatic day ↔ night cycle (toggle). City screen only; Drive is out of scope.
- Testing policy: light. Unit tests for core logic, plus one happy-path e2e. Playwright `--workers=1`, and only the touched specs.

---

### Task R1: 4-lane avenues
- **Data:** keep `CityState.roads: string[]` (cells). Width is derived. A 2-wide strip of road cells with parallel neighbours forms an avenue. This keeps old saves valid and the share format unchanged.
  - Alternative: store `avenues` explicitly, if derivation proves ambiguous at T-junctions or corners. Rule on it, and keep share and normalize backward compatible.
- **Tool:** the road tool gets a brush toggle: 🛣️ avenue (2×2 brush along the stroke) or 🛤 street (1 cell). Erase works the same way.
- **Rendering:** markings per cell from its neighbours:
  - avenue: a double yellow centre line between the two cells, dashed white lane dividers in the middle of each cell, curbs and sidewalks on the outer edges;
  - streets: unchanged;
  - 2×2 (or 2×1 / 1×2) intersection boxes with crosswalk stripes;
  - avenue × rail crossing: a 2-cell crossing, perpendicular only, like streets.
- **NPCs:** the lane graph gets 2 lanes per direction on avenues. Lane changes are optional; random turns happen at junctions. Streets keep 1 lane per direction. Mixed junctions (an avenue meeting a street) work.
- **Sample town:** main avenues form the grid, with side streets. It must still pass the sample-city validity test.
- Tests: unit tests for the avenue derivation, markings per cell, the NPC lanes on avenues, and old saves rendering as streets. One e2e: paint an avenue stroke, and the 2-wide road persists.

### Task E1: Horizon + time of day (City)
- **Sky:** a gradient sky dome (one cheap shader or vertex colours) whose colours come from the time of day. Fog matches the horizon colour.
- **Horizon:** a ring of low-poly LEGO-style mountains (stepped, brick-like) and pine forest beyond the city edge. It is one merged static mesh, created once and shared: no shadows, not pickable, not disposed by consumers. It must not be visible as a hard edge at max zoom-out.
- **Time of day:** four presets (☀️ morning, 🌞 noon, 🌅 sunset, 🌙 night). Each sets:
  - the sun or moon direction, colour and intensity;
  - the hemisphere and ambient light;
  - the sky colours;
  - the sun or moon disc on the sky dome;
  - the shadow direction.
- **Auto cycle** (toggle 🔄): about a 4–6 minute day that blends between the presets smoothly.
  - Keep cost low: update lights each frame (cheap), and avoid per-frame allocations.
  - The shadow map already re-renders.
  - Pause when the page is hidden.
- **Night:**
  - street lamps glow: emissive heads, plus a cheap glow sprite or a few point lights only near the camera; no per-lamp lights everywhere;
  - building windows glow: a trans or emissive window material at night, if cheap through the existing material kinds;
  - NPC cars get small headlight sprites.
  - Keep it cheap on tablets.
- **UI:** a time button in the City HUD `<TopRight>` (`city-time`) that cycles the presets, plus an auto toggle (`city-time-auto`). Both are persisted in useApp prefs. Reduced motion → no auto cycle.
- **Performance:** measure draw calls and triangles before and after. Target no more than +5 draw calls and +20k triangles for the horizon, with no frame-time regression.
- Tests: unit tests for preset interpolation and colours. One e2e: switching the time persists, and the scene's light colour changes (via `window.__bt`).

### Task P1: Battery and performance foundation + graphics settings (all scenes)
User request (2026-10-02): it must run well on phones and tablets without draining the battery. Propose the changes and add on/off settings for performance versus looks.

**Always on** (no visible loss):
- **Render on demand**: Workshop, Guided, the Maze editor and the static City (no NPCs, no auto time, no animated water) render only when something changes. Use R3F `frameloop="demand"` plus `invalidate()` on input, camera motion, animation and state changes. Animated scenes keep a loop at the chosen frame cap.
- **Frame cap**: Drive, the maze drive, and the City with animation run at a 30 or 60 fps cap from the settings. The default is 30 on phones and in battery mode.
- **DPR cap**: phones cap at 1.5, tablets at 1.75 (an S25 Ultra reports DPR 3.5+, which means 5× the pixels). Add adaptive DPR: drop one step when the frame time is too slow for about 3 s, and recover slowly.
- **Static shadows**: City buildings don't move, so set `shadowMap.autoUpdate = false` and re-render the shadow map only when the city, the camera framing, the time of day or the scene changes. NPCs cast no shadows, or only via cheap blobs.
- **Pause** the render loop when the page is hidden or a full-screen dialog covers the scene. The menu backdrop already does this; extend it to every scene.
- **Antialias** off when DPR ≥ 2 (the pixels already smooth edges).

**Settings ⚙️ "Đồ họa"** (in a settings dialog reachable from the main menu, for example next to 🎚️ sound), persisted in useApp prefs:
- **Preset**: 🔋 Tiết kiệm pin, ⚖️ Cân bằng, ✨ Đẹp nhất. The default is AUTO, from device signals: `deviceMemory`, `hardwareConcurrency`, a mobile UA or touch device, screen DPR, and the WebGL renderer string if available. Most phones get Cân bằng and low-end ones Tiết kiệm.
- **Individual toggles** under the preset; changing one marks the preset "Tùy chỉnh":
  - shadows: off, low or high;
  - resolution: Thấp, Vừa or Cao (the DPR cap);
  - frame rate: 30 or 60;
  - NPC density: off, ít or nhiều;
  - animated water;
  - horizon mountains;
  - auto day/night;
  - studs on far models (LOD).
- **Battery preset**: 30 fps, DPR 1, shadows off, NPC ít, water static, no auto day/night, simple horizon.
- **Đẹp nhất preset**: 60 fps, high DPR cap, shadows high, everything on.
- **Auto downgrade**: if the frame time stays bad for about 5 s in Cân bằng or Đẹp nhất, step down once and show a small dismissible toast ("Đã giảm đồ họa cho mượt hơn").
- A **dev overlay** (`?stats`) shows fps, draw calls, triangles and the DPR, to verify on real devices.

**Tests**: unit tests for the preset resolution from device signals, the toggles → render config mapping, and the auto-downgrade hysteresis. One e2e: change the preset, reload, and it persists; the canvas DPR follows it.
