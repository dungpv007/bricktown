# BrickTown — Role-play mini-games (Plan)

Goal: kids step into a role inside the city, in the style of BabyBus games:
- make and sell sushi;
- bake and decorate cakes;
- run the grocery till;
- answer fire and police calls.

User decisions (2026-10-02):
- **Entry**: tap a shop in the City. Placements whose template supports a game show a small ▶️ badge, and tapping the badge opens that shop's mini-game. There is also a "🎮 Nhập vai" card on the main menu for quick access.
- **First games**: the sushi restaurant, the bakery, the grocery cashier, and fire / police dispatch.
- **Rewards**: coins and collectible stickers. Coins unlock extra items (colours, figure styles, parts). There is no real money and there are no ads.
- **Look**: 3D LEGO scenes with a fixed camera (counter, minifigs, food built from bricks). Kid-sized tap and drag. It matches the game's style and reuses existing parts and figures.
- Testing policy: light. Unit tests for core game logic, plus one happy-path e2e per game. Playwright `--workers=1`, touched specs only.

## Global constraints for the games
- **Kid-first**:
  - no reading required: orders are picture bubbles, and hints are a pointing hand;
  - big targets (`--bt-touch`), no fail state;
  - mistakes give a gentle "try again" wobble;
  - every round ends happy, with confetti, coins and maybe a sticker;
  - a round is 3–5 customers, about 2–4 minutes.
- **Performance**:
  - each game is its own lazy chunk;
  - it uses P1's graphics settings: render on demand when idle, the frame cap while animating, DPR caps;
  - geometry is shared and baked;
  - it pauses when hidden.
- **Content**: food and props are built from catalog parts and print tiles, adding a few new parts or prints only if needed through brickGeometry and the print atlas. Figures use the existing presets.
- **Text**: all in vi and en, with sound effects through the existing sfx module and optional voice-less cues.
- **Saved data**: `SaveData.play?: { coins: number; stickers: string[]; unlocked: string[]; stats?: Record<gameId, {...}> }`. It is optional, normalised and clamped, so no schema bump is needed if normalize handles it. Coins are per save slot.

### Task G0: Role-play framework
- **Mode**: an app mode `play` with a `gameId` and an optional source placement id, so the kid returns to the same spot in the City. Each game is a lazy scene. Back returns to the City, or to the menu when started from the menu.
- **Registry**: `src/play/registry.ts` maps a gameId (`sushi`, `bakery`, `grocery`, `rescue`) to its icon, name, template ids that offer it (`sushi_restaurant` → sushi; `bakery` → bakery; `grocery` → grocery; `fire_station`, `police_station`, `police_hq` → rescue) and its lazy scene.
- **City entry**: a ▶️ badge floats above placements whose source template is in the registry. It shows only in City view mode, not while dragging or editing a selection, and is hidden when the kid toggles it off. Tapping the badge starts the game. In the selection action bar, a "🎮 Chơi" action also appears when the selected placement offers a game.
- **Menu card**: "🎮 Nhập vai" on the main menu opens a game picker (cards with icons) and a 🏅 sticker book.
- **Shared kit**: `src/play/kit/*`:
  - a fixed-camera scene shell (lighting, counter backdrop);
  - a customer queue (minifigs walking in and out along a path);
  - order bubbles (picture icons rendered from brick thumbnails);
  - a draggable item helper (pick up and drop on targets with snap);
  - a tap-to-act helper;
  - a progress bar;
  - the round flow (intro → customers → summary);
  - the summary screen (coins earned, sticker reveal, play again / back);
  - a pointing-hand hint after 5 s idle.
- **Rewards**:
  - the coin wallet with an animated counter in the top bar of games;
  - stickers: about 24, defined in `src/play/stickers.ts` (sushi chef, cake master, first customer, 10 customers…), unlocked by milestones;
  - a sticker book screen;
  - an **unlock shop** ("🛍️ Cửa hàng"): spend coins on a few extra colours, figure presets or hats, and fun parts. Locked items show a 🔒 with their price in the palettes and the figure editor. At least 6 starter unlockables.
- **Tests**: unit tests for the wallet, stickers, unlocks and normalisation; one e2e: tap the ▶️ on a sushi restaurant in the City, the play scene opens, Back returns to the City.

### Task G1: Sushi restaurant game (`sushi`)
- **Order**: a customer sits at the counter and orders by picture: maki (salmon / cucumber / tuna) or nigiri.
- **Making sushi**: the kid drags in order: seaweed sheet → rice → filling → taps "roll" (a rolling animation) → taps "cut" (it cuts into pieces) → drags the plate to the conveyor or the customer.
- **Serving**: the customer eats (an animation), pays coins and is happy. A wrong filling makes the customer shake their head; the kid fixes it, and nothing is lost.
- A round is 4 customers, with a speed-free difficulty.
- Food is built from bricks: rice is white plates, salmon is orange tiles, seaweed is a dark green plate, plus soy sauce and a wasabi dot.

### Task G2: Bakery game (`bakery`)
- **Order**: the customer shows a picture of a cake (shape, colour of frosting, toppings).
- **Making the cake**: the kid drags flour, egg and milk into a bowl → taps to mix → pours into a mould → into the oven (a short timer with a cute animation) → picks the frosting colour → drags toppings (strawberries, candles, sprinkles) → hands it over.
- Free decoration is allowed. A match with the order gives a bonus star.

### Task G3: Grocery cashier game (`grocery`)
- **Scanning**: a customer puts 2–5 items on the belt. The kid drags each item over the scanner (beep), and the total appears as big digits plus coin pictures.
- **Payment**: the customer pays; the kid taps coins to give change (simple, with a count-along). For young kids there is an option of no change, just tapping "pay".
- It teaches counting up to 20, with optional difficulty levels.

### Task G4: Fire and police dispatch (`rescue`)
- **Call**: at the station, a phone rings and the kid answers. A map bubble shows where: a building in the CURRENT city that is "on fire" (smoke particles) or a robber near a shop.
- **Drive**: the kid picks the fire truck or police car and drives in the existing Drive mode with a guide arrow to the target. It reuses the Drive scene with a mission overlay; the target is a placement in the current city.
- **On arrival**:
  - fire: hold a button to spray water at the flames until they go out;
  - police: tap the robber to catch them; the robber is a minifig that runs a little.
- Then the kid returns to the station: summary, coins, sticker.
- If the city has no suitable buildings, use a small built-in mission map.

## Execution
- G0 first, as the foundation.
- Then G1, G2, G3 and G4 run in parallel worktrees, each in its own `src/play/<game>/` folder and touching shared files only through the registry.
- Then a final review, light checks, merge and push.
